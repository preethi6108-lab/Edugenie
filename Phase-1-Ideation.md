# Phase 1 - Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
EduGenie: Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Problem Identified
Students may find complex topics difficult to understand and may need quick explanations, summaries, quizzes, and learning guidance.

## Proposed Solution
EduGenie uses generative AI to provide simple educational assistance through a single web application.

## Main Ideas
- Question answering
- Concept explanation
- Quiz generation
- Text summarization
- Learning recommendations

## Expected Benefit
Students can get quick and simple learning support from one platform.
